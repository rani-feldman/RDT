RDT user manual · Part 2: Operating from the GUI

A few keys are worth learning. F5 connects, F6 polls now, and F7 runs or stops polling. Control E adds an expression, control G charts the selection, and control L starts a CSV log. Control T shows statistics, and control one to four toggle the panels. F1 lists every shortcut.
