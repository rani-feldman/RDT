RDT user manual · Part 5: Files and where they are

Here’s where each file lives. The workspace and its files sit side by side, by default in your local app data folder, under R D T. That folder also holds your per-user GUI settings, the console history shared by the GUI and the command line, a cache of parsed ELF files, and the log. All of these are safe to delete except your workspaces. The programs themselves are installed under Programs, R D T.
