RDT user manual · Part 2: Operating from the GUI

To watch variables, click an MCU’s card to open its list of globals, and type in the search box to filter. Double-click a variable, or select several and press Add to watch list, or drag them onto a tab. Control E adds any expression. Each row shows the MCU’s coloured tag, the value, the type, the address, and the rate it’s actually read at. A value that just changed flashes yellow for a second.
