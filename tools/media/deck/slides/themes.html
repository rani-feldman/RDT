RDT user manual · Part 2: Operating from the GUI

You can arrange RDT the way you like. Control one to four show or hide the watch, scope, memory and console panels, and you can drag any panel to dock it elsewhere, stack it, or float it. View, Theme offers seven themes, including Studio dark, Islands and Instrument. View, Density gives compact or comfortable rows. The theme, layout and these settings are kept per user, not in the workspace.
