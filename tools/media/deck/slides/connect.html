RDT user manual · Part 2: Operating from the GUI

To connect, click the connect button or press F5. The button shows the state and the link. Blue means disconnected. Amber means connecting, and a click cancels. Green means the port or socket is open. Red means the link is down: the port broke, or the device answered none of the last three reads. While the link is down, writes are refused, the last values stay on screen in grey, and RDT reconnects by itself.
