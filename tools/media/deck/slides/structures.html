Structs and arrays

Open any struct or array to every field

  • Collapsed items are not read
  • Arrays show 100 cells at a time
  • Enums as names, char arrays as text
  • Follow a pointer, or watch one field alone
  • Several named watch tabs, all polling

RDT user manual · Part 2: Operating from the GUI

Structs and arrays open to every field and cell, to any depth. A collapsed struct or array isn’t read, so close what you aren’t looking at to keep the link free. Arrays show a hundred cells at a time. Enums show as names, char arrays as strings, and pointers with their target’s name. You can keep several named watch tabs, and every tab keeps polling, not only the one in front.
